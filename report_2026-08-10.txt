CORRECTIVE COMPLIANCE:
Hip Thrust (APT): 0 of 0 dedicated legs sessions this week -- but zero hip
thrusts logged in any session format at all. Anterior pelvic tilt is not
being corrected. Over time, untreated APT causes lower-back overextension
during squats and deadlifts, increasing disc compression risk. At 87kg with
body recomp as the goal, hip thrust is non-negotiable in every legs session.

Rear Delts (shoulder health): 0 sets this week vs the 8-set weekly minimum.
This is critical at 44. When front shoulder muscles (anterior and lateral
delts) are significantly stronger than rear delts, the shoulder joint is
pulled forward in the socket. This impingement position narrows the
subacromial space -- the small gap where the rotator cuff tendons pass. Under
load (bench press, overhead press), those tendons grind against the acromion.
The result is rotator cuff tendinopathy and eventually a partial tear, which
at 44 takes 6-12 months to recover from. Face pulls and rear delt flies are
injury prevention, not optional accessory work.

Core stabiliser (T8-T12 kyphosis): 0 of 2 sessions had dead bug, bird-dog,
or plank. The lower thoracic kyphosis (T8-T12) is a posture issue that worsens
under load without active stabilisation. Dead Bug and Bird-Dog train the deep
spinal stabilisers (multifidus, transverse abdominis) that hold the lumbar-
thoracic junction neutral during heavy compound lifts. Without this, the
kyphosis worsens under barbell squats and deadlifts.
