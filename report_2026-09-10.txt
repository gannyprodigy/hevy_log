CORRECTIVE COMPLIANCE

Hip Thrust hit rate this week: 0 of 0 (no pure Legs sessions were logged; the
leg work done in Full Body sessions did not include Hip Thrust in any session).
This is the mandatory APT corrective and it was absent all week. Anterior
pelvic tilt puts persistent tension on hip flexors and reduces glute activation
in squats and presses, increasing lumbar load. Hip Thrust directly counters
this by forcing posterior pelvic tilt under load; skipping it means the APT
is not being corrected regardless of how many leg exercises are logged.

Rear delt sets this week: 3 sets (vs 8-set target, 38% hit rate). This matters
specifically for a 44-year-old. As front delts (trained in every Bench and OHP
set) become disproportionately stronger than rear delts, the humeral head is
pulled slightly forward in the glenoid socket. Over time this compresses the
supraspinatus tendon against the acromion on every overhead movement -- this is
rotator cuff impingement. It progresses from sharp pain when lifting the arm to
a partial or full supraspinatus tear. The fix is straightforward: 8+ rear delt
sets per week (Face Pull or Rear Delt Fly) to pull the humeral head back into
correct position. This week was 3 sets -- not enough.

Core stabiliser presence: 0 of 3 sessions. Dead Bug or Bird-Dog must appear in
every session to address T8-T12 lower thoracic kyphosis. Three sessions this
week, zero correctives logged. This corrective is 2-3 minutes of work and was
skipped entirely.

Lateral Raise is flat at 10kg x 15 reps. This is the same weight logged on
10 Aug, 24 Aug, and 07 Sep -- at least 4 consecutive sessions with zero
progression. The V-taper deficit is specific to Ganesh's physique: the love
handles sit at the same horizontal level as the lats, which means the visual
width of the waist equals the visual width of the lats. The only way to improve

four weeks, Ganesh is ready to bump to 11.25kg or to increase reps to 17-18
before incrementing weight. The stall here is a direct brake on physique progress.
